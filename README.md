# CMPT 455 project
Introduction (Topic and Motivation)
Traffic collisions happen frequently in every major city, contributing to 1.35 million deaths annually worldwide (Babaoglu & Babaoglu, 2021). Data mining can help identify potentially dangerous intersection designs and highlight important safety priorities at intersections. Previous studies by Babaoglu & Babaoglu (2021), and Emu et al. (2022), mainly focus on fatalities, but only a small percentage of crashes result in fatalities. Instead, this study aims to investigate the relationship of intersection characteristics, traffic controls and environmental conditions with the number of casualties during traffic accidents.
